## Current generated-TB qualification

The first generated campaign was misrouted to a single shared AXI4-manager path despite the HLS contract having three independent AXI4 groups and an AXI4-Lite control-plane sequence. That dispatch and accepted-input capture have since been repaired. Reusable plan-selected bus schedules and non-destructive AXI4-Lite control probes closed the remaining required bins. The unchanged original now passes 137/137 with 20 ledger stimuli. The generated-TB **mutant qualification remains open**: only rows with a paired named failure and this full positive baseline earn `✓`.

The frozen HLS contract declares no AXI4 `FIXED`/`WRAP`, narrow transfer, unaligned address, or all-zero/sparse *AXI4 memory-port* write-strobe behavior. Those unsupported memory-port bins are not counted in the 137 required bins. AXI4-Lite control WSTRB zero/sparse is separate and was exercised through safe pointer-register probes. Lifecycle `job_lifecycle`/reset-abort coverage is absent from the resolved HLS plan even though lifecycle assertions are present; that remains a separate contract/qualification gap and is not silently credited by the 137/137 result.
